# Source and publication scope

Use official public SEC Company Facts and Submissions JSON endpoints and their EDGAR API documentation. These APIs provide submitted corporate financial facts and filing metadata without an API key. Current aggregates can change and are not immutable original-time captures. Financial facts and reported filing dates do not themselves establish a production point-in-time dataset or source truth.

Publish only checked minimal factual period, concept, unit, accession, filing/date and numeric tokens, with source hashes and original analysis. Keep raw JSON, company concept descriptions and labels, submission contact/address/EIN fields, corporate report prose, private personal data and credentials private. No investment advice, financial transaction, employer/client claim or SEC endorsement.

MIT covers original code, not blanket rights in third-party expressive filings or documentation. Independent program manuscripts may be public after source, numerical, privacy and rendering checks. Separate formal-publication projects including Azure optimization stay private.
